
CPU (Central Processing Unit) Issues


Symptoms

• System fails to boot or locks up immediately
• Random reboots, blue screen of death (BSOD), or application freezing under load
• Unrecognized dual-core/multi-core CPU after an upgrade
• Continuous error beep codes with no display output

Common Causes & Solutions

• Overheating & Failing Fans
	• Cause: Dust accumulation, dried thermal paste, or failed CPU cooler fan.
	• Solution: Clean dust from heatsinks/fans, reapply thermal paste, replace failing cooling fans, or upgrade to a higher-capacity cooler.
• Incorrect BIOS/UEFI Settings (Overclocking / Bus / Voltage)
	• Cause: High front-side bus (FSB), multiplier, or voltage settings causing instability.
	• Solution: Enter BIOS/UEFI and reset settings to default/optimized defaults.
• Outdated BIOS
	• Cause: Motherboard firmware does not recognize a newly installed CPU model.
	• Solution: Update the motherboard BIOS to the latest version.
• Physical Installation / Socket Mismatch
	• Cause: Bent CPU pins, improper seating, or incompatible socket type.
	• Solution: Verify socket compatibility, carefully reinstall the CPU, and check for bent pins.
